Look over yonder. Every 20 minutes, look at something about 20 feet (6 m) away for 20 seconds. It lets the focusing muscles in your eyes relax.

Work in focused stretches with a break after each one. Your eye breaks keep running alongside, and when one would land close to a focus break, the two are merged into a single interruption.

Lengths

A focus session counts active screen time only, so it waits while you are away, paused or outside office hours. When it ends, or when you stop it, your normal eye-break schedule carries on.

Strength

You can also turn the filter on or off from the tray menu. That choice lasts until the schedule next starts or ends.

Windows limits how strong a screen filter can be, so settings warmer than about 3400 K look the same.

Don't run another color filter (f.lux, Night Light, Night Shift) at the same time: they would fight over the screen.

Before a break

In the last five seconds a small countdown follows your cursor, so a break never catches you mid-sentence.

Your sounds

Built-in sounds are synthesized on the fly. Your own files (MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A, up to 8 MB) appear in every sound picker, for breaks and for each reminder, and stay on this computer.

Click a shortcut and press a new combination. Backspace clears it. During a break, press Esc twice to skip.

Run your own command when a break starts or ends: pause music, mute notifications, dim the lights.

Commands run in your shell with YONDER_EVENT (break-start, break-end, break-skip), YONDER_BREAK_KIND and YONDER_BREAK_SECS set.
